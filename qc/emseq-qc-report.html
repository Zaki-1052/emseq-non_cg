Assessment

The sequencing worked. Mapping rates are near-perfect, bisulfite conversion is clean, and sex assignments match labels. Even the shallowest sample (ctrl M1) has ~38x unique genomic coverage, well above the 10–30x typically needed for methylation calling.

Read depth is uneven. mut M2 got 3.4B reads (1.7B pairs); ctrl M1 got 1.0B (518M pairs). That's a 3.3x range for samples pooled at equal concentration. Probably a pooling quantification issue or index imbalance. The data's all usable, but you're paying for 118x unique coverage on mut M2 while ctrl M1 sits at 38x.
Duplication rates are on the high side. 31–39% across the board. The dups don't scale cleanly with depth (ctrl M2 at 1.4B reads has the highest dup rate at 38.9%, while mut M2 at 3.4B reads has 35.0%). Library complexity varied between preps. Estimated library sizes range from 1.2B to 3.5B fragments.
Conversion looks clean. Zero nonconverted reads on the phage Xp12 spike-in across all 8 samples. The mismatch rate of ~18.6% is standard for EM-seq (C→T conversions at unmethylated positions register as mismatches).
