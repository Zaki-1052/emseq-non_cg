Methodological decisions

edgeR over DSS
edgeR's quasi-likelihood F-test is calibrated for small n (4 per group). The initial CA-only analysis used DSS, which pinned dispersion at its 0.001 floor. Real overdispersion is 2 to 16x in variance. edgeR's empirical Bayes shrinkage gives prior df of 22.1, not 2.1. DESeq2 assumes gene-level counts. It doesn't handle the methylated-read-within-coverage structure that edgeR models via the offset term.
All-CH over CA-only
CHH + CHG combined gives about 3x more sites per gene body than CA alone. A CA-only pipeline exists for comparison with published mCA atlases. Both pipelines produce the same directional pattern.
Lambda as covariate, not pre-subtracted
The noise floor is 0.5 to 0.9%. The signal is about 1%. Pre-subtraction zeroed 30 to 85% of genes (depending on sample) and created a systematic artifact. Lambda rates correlate with genotype (r = +0.417). Mutant samples got more subtracted, producing an artificial genome-wide decrease. Including lambda as a covariate avoids count modification entirely.
Gene-body aggregation
Site-level differential mCH is underpowered. A 50 kb gene has about 3,050 CA sites. At 25x coverage, that gives about 76,000 observations per gene per sample.
FDR as binary gatekeeper
FDR is a threshold: pass or fail at 0.05. It's not a continuous weight or confidence score. This follows the ASA 2016 statement on p-values (Wasserstein and Lazar).

method Genome-wide CH DMR discovery with dmrseq. Processed per-chromosome to bound memory. testCovariate = "genotype", adjustCovariate = "sex", cutoff = 0.01, minNumRegion = 20. Lambda-corrected methylation counts. Runs parallel to steps 02 through 04.
