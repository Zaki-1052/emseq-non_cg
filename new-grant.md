Aim 1.1: Define the developmental sequence of H2Aub redistribution, DNMT3A occupancy, and 5mC/5hmC change. We will profile H2Aub (CUT&RUN), DNMT3A (ChIP-seq), and 5mC/5hmC (Biomodal evoC) in Bap1fl/fl,Math1-cre and littermate control cerebella at P7, P12, P21, and adult (P60), spanning the window in which granule neurons complete postmitotic maturation. At each timepoint, we will test for differential methylation using the modality XPLR analysis pipeline, which fits a binomial logistic regression per region and tests the genotype coefficient by Wald test, with sex as a covariate. The model tests 5mC and 5hmC independently across six GENCODE vM25 annotation classes (gene bodies, promoters, CpG islands, CpG shores, CpG shelves, and TSS regions), with significance defined by Benjamini-Hochberg FDR at q < 0.05. We will classify genes significant in both 5mC and 5hmC by the direction of change in each context: we predict that loci that gain H2Aub will show coordinated 5mC increase and 5hmC decrease — the signature of impaired TET-mediated demethylation — while loci that lose H2Aub will show the reciprocal pattern. To ask whether DNMT3A occupancy and methylation change follow H2Aub redistribution rather than co-occur with or precede it, we will run the differential analyses at each timepoint independently and record the earliest timepoint at which each mark (H2Aub, DNMT3A, 5mC/5hmC) first reaches significance at each gene. We will test by permutation whether the predicted ordering — H2Aub before DNMT3A, DNMT3A before methylation change — occurs more frequently than expected by chance.

Aim 1.2: Profile non-CG methylation (mCH) across the same time course. Because mCH is a neuronally enriched, DNMT3A-dependent mark rarely profiled at this resolution in a disease model, we will apply Enzymatic Methyl-seq to the same P7–adult time course to determine whether mCH accrual is redirected toward loci that gain H2Aub and DNMT3A occupancy, and lost at loci that lose them, in parallel with the CG methylation changes captured by evoC. We will quantify mCH at the gene-body level by aggregating all CH (CHH and CHG) sites within each GENCODE vM25 protein-coding gene body per sample. We will test differential gene-body mCH using the edgeR quasi-likelihood F-test (Benjamini-Hochberg FDR q < 0.05), with genotype and sex as biological covariates and log(total_coverage) as a per-gene offset. We will incorporate the per-sample conversion rate at lambda phage CH sites as a technical covariate to adjust for between-sample differences in enzymatic conversion efficiency.

Rigor and Reproducibility. The genome-wide experiments proposed here are, by design, simple two-condition comparisons that are well suited to rigorous execution in mice. In every mouse experiment, mutants are compared to Cre-negative or, given the recessive nature of Bap1 loss, flox-negative littermate controls generated from the same crosses and housed together, so that genotype is the only systematic variable. Both sexes are included at each developmental timepoint, with sex incorporated as a covariate in all differential models and tested explicitly for genotype-by-sex interaction. Sample sizes follow our completed preliminary studies, in which n=6 biological replicates per genotype yielded well-powered CUT&RUN differential analyses and n=7 sufficed for RNA-seq. We will maintain n≥6 per genotype per timepoint for CUT&RUN and RNA-seq, and n=4 per genotype per timepoint for methylation profiling (evoC and EM-seq). 
Analyses use established, publicly available pipelines already validated in our hands — SEACR for peak calling, DiffBind for differential binding, DESeq2 for transcript abundance, the modality XPLR binomial logistic regression for CG differential methylation, and the edgeR quasi-likelihood F-test for non-CG differential methylation — with statistical thresholds (FDR<0.05) and spike-in normalization (per-sample lambda phage conversion rate as a model covariate for EM-seq) specified in advance. evoC reads will be processed through the Biomodal DUET v1.5.0 pipeline (Fullgrabe et al. 2023). EM-seq reads will be aligned with bwa-meth and methylation called with MethylDackel using the NEB EM-seq Nextflow pipeline (Vaisvila et al. 2021). All antibodies and CUT&RUN affinity reagents are validated for CUT&RUN and show obvious gebotype difference, which are highly unlikely for non-secific reagents. All sequencing data will be deposited in GEO with processing code released publicly.
